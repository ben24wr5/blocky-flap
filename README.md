# 🐔 Blocky Flap

A Minecraft-style Flappy Bird game in a single HTML file, with a classic iOS 6-style interface.

## [▶ Play Blocky Flap](https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/ben24wr5/blocky-flap/blob/main/index.html)

**https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/ben24wr5/blocky-flap/blob/main/index.html**

## Features

- **Minecraft pixel-art world:** grass, dirt, stone and cobblestone pillars, square clouds and scrolling grass ground, all drawn in code.
- **iOS 6-style interface:** glossy blue navigation bar, linen-textured start card, glossy buttons and a "You Died!" alert box.
- **4 bird skins:** Chicken (default), Pig, Steve and Creeper. Pick one in ⚙️ Settings → Bird.
- **16 Home Screen icons:** choose your Home Screen icon (any skin on sky, grass, pipes or night) in ⚙️ Settings → App Icon, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Sound effects:** wing flap, point ding, hit, button clicks and a death sound for each skin (cluck, oink, oof, and a Creeper hiss and boom). Turn them off in Settings.
- **Best score saved** on your device, plus a Reset Best Score option.
- Works on iPhone and desktop. Tap, click or press Space to flap.

## Add to Home Screen (iPhone)

1. Open the play link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Blocky Flap now has its own icon on your Home Screen.

On Android (Chrome), tap **⋮** and choose **Add to Home screen**.
